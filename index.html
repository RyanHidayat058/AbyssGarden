How to Play Abyss Garden

Quick Overview

Use WASD to swim, aim with the mouse, and use Space or Left Click to till sand, sow glowing spores, inject nutrients, and harvest. Visit Barnaby the Crab to trade!

Subsea Farming Steps

  1. Clear Seabed: Select Sand Shovel (1) and dig wild sand into a fertile plot.
  2. Sow Spores: Select Kelp, Coral, Oyster, or Jelly-Shroom (4-6) to plant.
  3. Nutrient Boost: Select Nutrient Injector (2) to accelerate growth 2.2x!
  4. Harvest: When fully grown and glowing, use your hand or net (3) to harvest!
  5. Trade: Visit Barnaby the Crab at the coral outpost to sell harvests and buy upgrades.
  6. Oxygen: Replenish your oxygen tank at bubbling Hydrothermal Vents.
